Cover = (Current + In-transit) ÷ monthly demand. Historical demand = 12-month average of the Historical Data sheet (returns counted as 0); Market demand = Market Demand sheet. Items with stock but no demand are Critical. Segment = number of months (last 12, excluding the months listed above) with at least one sale in the past sales file, for the selected zone/branch. Strangers are make-to-order: no reorder qty. Stock status is always national. Tap a row to see its monthly sales.
